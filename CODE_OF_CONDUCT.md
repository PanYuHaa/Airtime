# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Airtime community a harassment-free, constructive, and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive engineering culture:

- Focusing code reviews on technical correctness, reproducibility, and maintainability rather than personal criticism.
- Providing concrete evidence (benchmarks, reproduction steps, data) when proposing changes or raising concerns.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and graciously accepting constructive feedback.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, personal attacks, publishing private data or videos without consent, or deliberately introducing malicious code.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Concerns may be reported directly to the repository maintainers.
